# Olive v1.2.0 — What's New

Olive now supports the wider birth journey while keeping the labor timer fast and calm.

- Keep a private care card with the facts you choose to record.
- Save questions for your provider without automated medical answers.
- Assign practical responsibilities and explicitly review which ones you share with a partner.
- Record appointments, support, reminders, recovery notes, and personal milestones through the first 12 weeks postpartum.
- Back up and restore journey information alongside contraction history.
- Move between preparing, labor, postpartum, and archived phases only when you choose.
- Continue using the core timer offline, including after reopening the installed app.

Olive is an informational timing and communication tool, not a medical device. Follow the instructions from your healthcare professional and use local emergency services when urgent help is needed.
