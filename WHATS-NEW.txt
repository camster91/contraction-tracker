# Olive v1.3.0 — What's New

Olive is now private by default, and available only in the App Store and Play Store.

- Partner sharing and its relay server are gone. Every record — contractions, birth journey, care card, notes — lives only on your device.
- Keep using the birth journey: a private care card, provider questions, practical responsibilities, and a factual first-12-weeks postpartum timeline.
- Back up and restore your full history with a single file, and hand an objective care summary to your provider right from the timer screen.
- The web app is retired. Updates arrive through the App Store or Play Store.

Olive is an informational timing and communication tool, not a medical device. Follow the instructions from your healthcare professional and use local emergency services when urgent help is needed.
