# Olive v1.0.0 — What's New

Welcome to Olive. This is the first public release.

**Contraction timer**
- One-tap Start/Stop
- 5-1-1 pattern detection — the app tells you when to call your provider
- History with frequency chart
- Notes, intensity, tags, pain locations per contraction

**Share with your partner**
- Send a 6-character code via text
- They open https://contractions.ashbi.ca, enter the code, see your contractions in real-time
- No app install required for your partner

**Hospital bag checklist**
- Pre-loaded list
- Check off as you pack
- Persists between sessions

**Privacy**
- Your data lives on your device
- Zero analytics, zero tracking
- The relay only sees an encrypted session ID during active shares
- Voice audio is processed on-device

**On-device**
- Screen stays awake while timing
- Voice control: "start" / "stop" hands-free
- Works offline

Read the full privacy policy: https://contractions.ashbi.ca/privacy

Built by a dad for his wife — now for you.
