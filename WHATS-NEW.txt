# Olive v1.1.0 — What's New

This release keeps Olive focused during labor and makes care-team handoff clearer.

- Save the contraction timing instructions provided by your care team.
- Add your provider or care-team name and phone number for a direct call action.
- Share an objective timing summary with recent averages, entries, and notes.
- Pattern notices now describe the recorded timing without diagnosing labor.
- Partner sharing stays on the main screen; preparation tools are organized under More tools.
- Shared session status changes are always controlled by you.
- Offline reopening, secure sharing, and accessibility improvements from v1.0.1 remain included.

Olive is an informational timing and communication tool, not a medical device. Follow the instructions from your healthcare professional and use local emergency services when urgent help is needed.
