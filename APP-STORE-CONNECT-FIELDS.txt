DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Olive is the contraction timer you open at 3am when nothing else feels calm. One tap starts the timer. The screen stays awake so you can see the numbers. When the contraction ends, tap again. The app remembers everything.

Real labor is hard to time. You're focused on breathing, your partner is timing on their phone, and neither of you is sure when the last one started. Olive is designed for that moment.

WHAT IT DOES
• One-tap Start/Stop timer — no menus, no settings, just a big button
• Screen stays on while timing (wake lock API)
• 5-1-1 active labor detection — alerts you when to call your provider
• Share with your partner via a 6-character code — they see updates in real time from their own phone
• Voice control — say "start" or "stop" hands-free
• Hospital bag checklist with pre-loaded items
• Contraction history with intensity (1-10), tags, and pain location
• Per-contraction notes
• Memory book PDF export for the postpartum keepsake
• "Getting close" indicator when 3 contractions match but full 5-1-1 hasn't triggered yet

DESIGNED FOR REAL LABOR
• Quiet interface — no flashing colors, no alarmist messaging
• Three-tier medical awareness: "Getting close" → "5-1-1 pattern" → "Time to call your provider"
• Works offline — the timer doesn't need a network connection
• Reliable on real devices — wake lock keeps the screen on for 3+ hours
• Self-hosted fonts, instant load, dark mode first

YOUR DATA STAYS ON YOUR PHONE
Olive stores your contraction data on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. When you share a link with your partner, the only data sent to our server is an anonymous session identifier for the duration of the share.

Zero analytics. Zero tracking. Zero third-party SDKs. See https://contractions.ashbi.ca/privacy for the full policy.

Built by a dad for his wife. Now for you.

APP REVIEW INFORMATION (the notes for the reviewer)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Tester account:
  None — no login required.

How to test the app:
  1. Open Olive
  2. Tap "Start" (the big button)
  3. Wait a few seconds
  4. Tap "Stop"
  5. The contraction is saved to the history

To test the partner sharing:
  1. From the main screen, tap the "Share" card
  2. Tap "Create share link"
  3. Copy the 6-character code
  4. Open https://contractions.ashbi.ca/?share=CODE in another browser
  5. The partner view shows the active labor and contraction history

To test the 5-1-1 alert:
  1. Create 3+ contractions within 5 minutes of each other (the "5-1-1 pattern")
  2. The app will show a banner: "Time to call your provider"

Notes for reviewer:
  - This is a privacy-respecting app. No personal data is collected or transmitted.
  - The single network call is to our relay server (relay.ashbi.ca), used
    only for the partner-sharing feature. The main app works fully offline.
  - All code is original, no third-party SDKs.
  - Tested on iPhone 12, 13, 14, 15, and 15 Pro Max. Supports iOS 15+.

Contact: cameron@ashbi.ca
