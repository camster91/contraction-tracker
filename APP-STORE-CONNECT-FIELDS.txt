DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Olive is a private birth-journey companion with the contraction timer you open at 3am when nothing else feels calm. One tap starts the timer. The screen stays awake so you can see the numbers. When the contraction ends, tap again. The app remembers everything.

Real labor is hard to time. You're focused on breathing, your partner is timing on their phone, and neither of you is sure when the last one started. Olive is designed for that moment.

Before and after labor, Olive keeps a private care card, provider questions, practical responsibilities, and a factual first-12-weeks postpartum timeline. You choose the phase, and only reviewed responsibilities can be included in a partner link.

WHAT IT DOES
• One-tap Start/Stop timer — no menus, no settings, just a big button
• Screen stays on while timing (wake lock API)
• Provider-configurable timing reminders with an optional care-team call action
• Share with your partner via a 12-character code — they see updates in real time from their own phone
• Voice control — say "start" or "stop" hands-free
• Hospital bag checklist with pre-loaded items
• Contraction history with intensity (1-10), tags, and pain location
• Per-contraction notes
• Memory book PDF export for the postpartum keepsake
• Objective frequent-pattern indicator without diagnostic claims

DESIGNED FOR REAL LABOR
• Quiet interface — no flashing colors, no alarmist messaging
• User-controlled care-plan reminder with clear limitations and provider handoff
• Works offline — the timer doesn't need a network connection
• Reliable on real devices — wake lock keeps the screen on for 3+ hours
• Self-hosted fonts, instant load, dark mode first

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Olive stores your session on your device. If you choose to create a share link, the contraction session and optional activity-feed content are sent through the Olive relay so people with the link can follow along. Shared data expires automatically and can be revoked by the host.

Care-card details, provider questions, private responsibilities, and journey notes stay off the relay. Zero analytics. Zero tracking. Zero third-party SDKs. See https://contractions.ashbi.ca/privacy for the full policy.

Built by a dad for his wife. Now for you.

APP REVIEW INFORMATION (the notes for the reviewer)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Tester account:
  None — no login required.

How to test the app:
  1. Open Olive
  2. Tap "Start" (the big button)
  3. Wait a few seconds
  4. Tap "Stop"
  5. The contraction is saved to the history

To test the partner sharing:
  1. From the main screen, tap the "Share" card
  2. Tap "Create share link"
  3. Copy the 12-character code
  4. Open https://contractions.ashbi.ca/?share=CODE in another browser
  5. The partner view shows the shared contraction history

To test the care-plan reminder:
  1. Open Settings and save the timing instructions provided by the reviewer's care team.
  2. Enter a care-team name and phone number.
  3. Record a sustained pattern matching the saved interval, duration, and window.
  4. Olive shows an observational reminder and the saved call action; it does not diagnose labor.

Notes for reviewer:
  - Sharing is optional. If enabled, contraction/session data and optional activity-feed content are transmitted to the Olive relay as disclosed in the privacy policy.
  - Network requests to relay.ashbi.ca are used only for the optional
    partner-sharing feature. The main timer and local history work offline.
  - The app uses the open-source libraries listed in its packaged dependencies.
  - Tested on iPhone 12, 13, 14, 15, and 15 Pro Max. Supports iOS 15+.
