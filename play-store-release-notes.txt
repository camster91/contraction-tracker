# Olive v1.2.1 — Play Store release notes

Olive now supports the wider birth journey while keeping the contraction timer fast, calm, and offline-first.

What's new:
- See partner-completed responsibilities update while Olive stays open, without sending private tasks to the relay.
- Keep a private care card and provider-question list.
- Assign practical responsibilities and explicitly choose which ones a partner may view and complete.
- Record appointments, support, reminders, recovery notes, and milestones through the first 12 weeks postpartum.
- Back up journey information with contraction history.
- Change preparation, labor, postpartum, and archived phases only when you choose.
- Continue timing offline, including after reopening the installed app.

Olive is an informational timing and communication tool, not a medical device. Follow the instructions from your healthcare professional and use local emergency services when urgent help is needed.
