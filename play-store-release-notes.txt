# Olive v1.1.0 — Play Store release notes

Olive is a private, offline-first contraction timer with optional real-time partner sharing.

What's new:
- Save timing instructions from your care team.
- Add a provider name and phone number for a direct call action.
- Share an objective care summary with recent timing, averages, notes, and intensity.
- Timing notices now report observations without diagnosing labor.
- Partner sharing remains prominent while preparation tools are organized under More tools.
- Shared session status is always controlled by the user.
- Includes secure sharing, offline reopening, accessibility, and security improvements.

Olive is an informational timing and communication tool, not a medical device. Follow the instructions from your healthcare professional and use local emergency services when urgent help is needed.
