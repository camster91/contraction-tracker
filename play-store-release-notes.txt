# Olive v1.0.0 — Play Store release notes

## What is Olive?
Olive is a contraction timer for expecting couples. Track contractions in real time with one tap, see the 5-1-1 pattern detection, and share a live link with your partner so they can follow along from their own phone.

## What's new in v1.0.0
- One-tap Start/Stop contraction timer
- Screen stays awake while timing
- 5-1-1 active labor detection — alerts you when to call your provider
- Share with partner — one link, valid 7 days, real-time updates
- Voice control — say "start" or "stop" hands-free
- Hospital bag checklist
- Contraction history with intensity, tags, and pain location
- Per-contraction notes
- Memory book PDF export for postpartum keepsake
- On-device only — your data never leaves your phone
- Zero analytics, zero tracking, zero third-party SDKs

## Privacy
Olive stores sessions on your device by default. If you create a share link, shared contraction data and optional activity-feed content are sent through the Olive relay, expire automatically, and can be revoked by the host. See https://contractions.ashbi.ca/privacy for the full policy.

## Designed for real labor
- Quiet interface — no flashing colors, no alarmist messaging
- Three-tier medical awareness: "Getting close" → "5-1-1 pattern" → "Time to call your provider"
- Works offline — the timer doesn't need a network connection
- Reliable on real devices — wake lock keeps the screen on for 3+ hours

Built by a dad for his wife. Now for you.
