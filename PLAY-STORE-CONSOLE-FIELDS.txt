FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Olive is the contraction timer you open at 3am when nothing else feels calm. One tap starts the timer. The screen stays awake so you can see the numbers. When the contraction ends, tap again. The app remembers everything.

Real labor is hard to time. You're focused on breathing, your partner is timing on their phone, and neither of you is sure when the last one started. Olive is designed for that moment.

WHAT IT DOES
• One-tap Start/Stop timer — no menus, no settings, just a big button
• Screen stays on while timing (wake lock API)
• 5-1-1 active labor detection — alerts you when to call your provider
• Share with your partner via a 6-character code — they see updates in real time from their own phone
• Voice control — say "start" or "stop" hands-free
• Hospital bag checklist with pre-loaded items
• Contraction history with intensity (1-10), tags, and pain location
• Per-contraction notes
• Memory book PDF export for the postpartum keepsake
• "Getting close" indicator when 3 contractions match but full 5-1-1 hasn't triggered yet

DESIGNED FOR REAL LABOR
• Quiet interface — no flashing colors, no alarmist messaging
• Three-tier medical awareness: "Getting close" → "5-1-1 pattern" → "Time to call your provider"
• Works offline — the timer doesn't need a network connection
• Reliable on real devices — wake lock keeps the screen on for 3+ hours
• Self-hosted fonts, instant load, dark mode first

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Olive stores your session on your device. If you choose to create a share link, the contraction session and optional activity-feed content are sent through the Olive relay so people with the link can follow along. Shared data expires automatically and can be revoked by the host.

Zero analytics. Zero tracking. Zero third-party SDKs. See https://contractions.ashbi.ca/privacy for the full policy.

Built by a dad for his wife. Now for you.

DATA SAFETY (the new 2024 form)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Q: Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?
A: Yes. The optional sharing feature collects data on the Olive relay.

Declare these collected data types for App functionality:
- Health info: contraction timestamps, durations, intensity, notes, tags,
  pain-location markers, stage, and timer state
- Personal info, Messages, Photos and videos, and other user-generated content:
  optional display names, text, reactions, and images posted to a shared session
- User IDs: random share/session and browser client identifiers

Collection is optional because it occurs only when the user creates or joins
a shared session. The app does not sell data or use it for advertising.

Q: Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?
A: Yes. Shared-session traffic uses HTTPS.

Q: Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted?
A: Yes. Hosts can revoke a share in the app, shares auto-delete after expiry,
and the privacy policy provides a support channel for deletion help.

Q: Is your app designed for children?
A: No, the app is for expecting parents (18+).

Q: Does your app contain ads?
A: No.

Q: Does your app have in-app purchases?
A: No. The v1.0.1 build has no in-app purchase implementation.

Q: Does your app use any data for tracking?
A: No, the app does not track users.

Q: Does your app target children under 13?
A: No.


HEALTH APP DECLARATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Is this a health app? YES — it times medical events (contractions)
  and provides medical information (5-1-1 labor pattern guidance).

Does it read or write health data? YES — it stores contraction
  history locally (intensity, duration, timestamps).

Does it connect to medical devices? NO.

Compliance: This is a wellness/utility app, not a regulated medical
  device. The 5-1-1 pattern is general public health knowledge, not
  a diagnostic tool. We do not claim to diagnose or treat any
  medical condition. The privacy policy explicitly states "Olive is
  not a medical device."
