SHORT DESCRIPTION (max 80 chars)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
A private birth journey and a calm labor timer — all on your device.


FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Olive is a private birth-journey companion with the contraction timer you open at 3am when nothing else feels calm. One tap starts the timer. The screen stays awake so you can see the numbers. When the contraction ends, tap again. Your records are saved locally; export a backup before changing devices.

Real labor is hard to time. You're focused on breathing, your partner is timing on their phone, and neither of you is sure when the last one started. Olive is designed for that moment.

Before and after labor, Olive keeps a private care card, provider questions, practical responsibilities, and a factual first-12-weeks postpartum timeline. You choose the phase, and everything stays on your phone.

WHAT IT DOES
• One-tap Start/Stop timer — no menus, no settings, just a big button
• Requests that the screen stays on while timing, when your device permits it
• Provider-configurable timing reminders with an optional care-team call action
• Hospital bag checklist with pre-loaded items
• Contraction history with intensity (1-10), tags, and pain location
• Per-contraction notes
• Optional reminders based on timing instructions you enter from your care team
• One-file backup and restore for your full history
• Care summary handoff to your provider, right from the timer screen

DESIGNED FOR REAL LABOR
• Quiet interface — no flashing colors, no alarmist messaging
• User-controlled care-plan reminder with clear limitations and provider handoff
• Works offline — the timer doesn't need a network connection
• The screen stays awake while timing when your device permits it
• Self-hosted fonts, instant load, dark mode first

PRIVATE BY DEFAULT
Everything you record — contractions, journey details, notes, care-team contacts — stays on your device. Olive makes no network requests, has no account system, and contains zero analytics, zero tracking, and no advertising or analytics SDKs. See https://olive.ashbi.ca/privacy for the full policy.

Built by a dad for his wife. Now for you.

DATA SAFETY (the new 2024 form)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Q: Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types?
A: No. As of v1.3.0 the app makes no network requests and sends
   nothing off-device. All data (contraction history, journey details,
   notes, contacts) is stored locally and never transmitted.

Q: Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?
A: Not applicable — no data is collected or transmitted.

Q: Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted?
A: Local records are removed by uninstalling Olive or clearing app storage.
   Users must separately remove any backups or summaries they exported.
   There is no server account or remotely collected data to delete.

Q: Is your app designed for children?
A: No, the app is for expecting parents (18+).

Q: Does your app contain ads?
A: No.

Q: Does your app have in-app purchases?
A: No. The v1.3.0 build has no in-app purchase implementation.

Q: Does your app use any data for tracking?
A: No, the app does not track users.

Q: Does your app target children under 13?
A: No.


HEALTH APP DECLARATION
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Is this a health app? YES — it records user-entered contraction timing
  on the user's device.

Does it read or write health data? YES — it stores contraction
  history locally (intensity, duration, timestamps). The data
  never leaves the device.

Does it connect to medical devices? NO.

Compliance: This is an informational timing and communication tool,
  not a medical device. Reminder thresholds are saved by the user from
  their care team's instructions. Olive reports observations and does
  not claim to diagnose or treat any medical condition.
