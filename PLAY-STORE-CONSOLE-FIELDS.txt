FULL DESCRIPTION (max 4000 chars)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
Olive is a private contraction timer and birth-journey organizer. Tap Start when a contraction begins and Stop when it ends. Review the recorded duration and interval, add a missed contraction, or correct an earlier entry.

YOUR TIMING, IN ONE PLACE
Record contraction timing, optional intensity, notes and tags. Review a factual history and export a care summary when you want to share it.

YOUR CARE TEAM'S PLAN
Timing reminders are off by default. Enter instructions from your own care team and choose whether to enable a saved reminder. Olive reports observed timing; it does not diagnose labor or tell you whether it is safe to wait.

BEFORE AND AFTER LABOR
Keep a private care card, questions for your provider, practical responsibilities, a hospital-bag checklist and postpartum notes. Log exam measurements reported to you by your care team without interpreting progress.

LOCAL AND OFFLINE
Core timing and records work offline. No account, ads or analytics are required. Olive stores your records on your device and does not send them to the developer. Export a backup before changing devices or uninstalling the app. Files you choose to save or share may leave the device through the destination you select.

Olive organizes your observations and notes. It does not replace professional care. Follow your care team's instructions and contact them when you have concerns.

Privacy: https://olive.ashbi.ca/privacy/
