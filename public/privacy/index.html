Privacy Policy

Effective June 2026 App version 1.0.0 Contact contractions.ashbi.ca

Overview

Olive is a contraction timer app designed for expecting couples. It tracks labor contractions in real time and syncs data between partners' devices. This policy explains what data is collected, how it is handled, and your rights regarding that data.

Olive is built on a fundamental principle: your health data belongs to you.

Data we collect

Session data (local only)

When you use Olive, the following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Contraction timestamps (start time, end time, duration)
  • Contraction intervals (time between contractions)
  • Session start/end times
  • Labor stage indicators derived from contraction patterns
  • Optional notes, tags, intensity, pain-location markers you add yourself
  • People you choose to add to your care team (name, phone, email — entered by you)
  • Hospital info (name, address, doctor, etc. — entered by you)

This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly share it with your partner.

Multi-device sync data

When you choose to share your session with your partner using the share function, Olive generates a short-lived, encrypted session token. This token is passed through our relay server (relay.ashbi.ca) to deliver real-time updates to your partner's device.

What the relay sees:

  • An encrypted token containing only a session ID and connection metadata
  • No health data, no contraction data, no names or identifying information

What the relay does NOT see or store:

  • Contraction timestamps or durations
  • Any health information
  • Your name or device identifiers
  • Your labor stage or pattern detection results

The relay is stateless. It passes tokens and closes. It logs nothing meaningful and retains nothing beyond the active session.

Voice control data

If you use the voice control feature, audio is processed on-device using the Web Speech API. No voice recordings are transmitted to any server. No audio leaves your phone.

No analytics, no tracking

Olive contains zero analytics SDKs, zero tracking pixels, zero third-party data collectors. We do not know how many people use the app, what features you use, or when you use it.

No ads

Olive contains no advertising, no sponsored content, and no in-app purchases beyond the initial purchase price of $1.99.

How we use data

What we use:

  • Sync tokens — to deliver real-time updates between partners' devices during an active shared session
  • Local session data — to display contraction history and pattern detection within the app on your device

What we don't use:

  • We do not use your data to build profiles.
  • We do not sell, license, or share your data with any third party.
  • We do not use your data for research, marketing, or product improvement in any form that identifies you.

Data retention

  • Local data is stored on your device only. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's local storage.
  • Sync tokens are short-lived and expire when the session ends. The relay server retains no session data after disconnection.
  • No cloud backup of session data is performed by Olive. If you back up your device via iCloud, Google Drive, or any other platform, your Olive session data may be included in that backup. That is a function of your device settings, not Olive.

Data sharing with third parties

Olive shares nothing with any third party. There are no third-party SDKs embedded in this app, no analytics services, no crash reporters, no ad networks.

The only external communication the app makes is to our own relay server (relay.ashbi.ca) — which receives and delivers encrypted sync tokens during active shared sessions only.

Children's privacy

Olive is not designed for use by children under 13. We do not collect data from children.

Your rights

You own your data. At any time, you can:

  • Delete all local data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from your device settings.
  • End a shared session at any time, which invalidates the sync token immediately.
  • Export your data as a JSON backup file at any time.
  • Request deletion of any data we might theoretically hold (sync tokens are already stateless; contact us for edge cases).

To exercise any rights, contact us at the support URL above.

Security

  • Sync tokens are encrypted in transit via HTTPS / TLS 1.2+.
  • The relay server accepts connections only over HTTPS.
  • Session tokens are short-lived and invalidated when the session ends.
  • No health data is stored on the relay server at any time.
  • Local data is stored in the browser's IndexedDB / Capacitor Preferences, which is sandboxed per-app on the device.
  • Voice audio is processed on-device and never leaves the phone.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be posted at the same location with a new effective date. Significant changes will be communicated within the app.

Governing law
