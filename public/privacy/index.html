Olive
Privacy Policy

Effective August 7, 2026 App version 1.0.1 Contact contractions.ashbi.ca

Overview

Olive is a contraction timer for expecting parents and their support people. It can store information on your device and, only when you turn on sharing, send selected session information through the Olive relay so another device can view or contribute to the session. This policy explains what data is handled and why.

Olive is built on a fundamental principle: your health data belongs to you.

Data we collect

Session data (local only)

When you use Olive, the following data is stored locally on your device:

  • Contraction timestamps (start time, end time, duration)
  • Contraction intervals (time between contractions)
  • Session start/end times
  • Labor stage indicators derived from contraction patterns
  • Optional notes, tags, intensity, pain-location markers you add yourself
  • People you choose to add to your care team (name, phone, email — entered by you)
  • Hospital info (name, address, doctor, etc. — entered by you)

Care-team contacts and hospital details stay on your device. Contraction and session data stays on your device unless you explicitly create a shared session.

Multi-device sync data

When you create a share link, Olive sends data to our relay server (relay.ashbi.ca) so people with the share code can receive updates. Sharing is optional and is initiated by you.

Data stored for a shared session may include:

  • A random share code, session identifier, sharing mode, current stage, creation/expiry times, and limited audit events
  • Contraction start/end timestamps, durations, intensity, notes, tags, pain-location markers, and current timer state
  • Optional activity-feed content, including display names, text, reactions, and images you choose to post
  • A random browser client identifier used to keep activity-feed names consistent

Data not uploaded by the sharing feature:

  • Your phone contacts or address book
  • Your hospital and care-team details
  • Your exported JSON or PDF files

The relay application does not store IP addresses in its session database. Network infrastructure necessarily processes IP addresses and request metadata to deliver and protect the service.

Voice control data

If you enable voice control, Olive uses the speech-recognition service supplied by your browser or operating system. Processing may occur on-device or through that platform's speech service, depending on the device and settings. Olive does not record, retain, or send voice audio to the Olive relay.

No analytics, no tracking

Olive contains zero analytics SDKs, zero tracking pixels, zero third-party data collectors. We do not know how many people use the app, what features you use, or when you use it.

No ads

Olive contains no advertising or sponsored content.

How we use data

What we use:

  • Shared-session data — to create the link, synchronize updates, show the activity feed, limit abuse, and let you revoke the share
  • Local session data — to display contraction history and pattern detection within the app on your device

What we don't use:

  • We do not use your data to build profiles.
  • We do not sell or license your data.
  • We do not use your data for research, marketing, or product improvement in any form that identifies you.

Data retention

  • Local data is stored on your device only. You can delete it at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing the app's local storage.
  • Shared-session data is deleted automatically after the share expires. The default share period is seven days.
  • Revoking a share immediately removes its contraction data, activity-feed content, and client-name bindings from the relay. Minimal revocation metadata may remain until the share expires so the relay can continue rejecting the revoked code.
  • No cloud backup of session data is performed by Olive. If you back up your device via iCloud, Google Drive, or any other platform, your Olive session data may be included in that backup. That is a function of your device settings, not Olive.

Data sharing with third parties

Olive does not send data to advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting services. Shared-session data is sent only to our Olive relay (relay.ashbi.ca) and to people who have the share link. Your device's speech-recognition provider may process voice input when you enable voice control.

Children's privacy

Olive is not designed for use by children under 13. We do not collect data from children.

Your rights

You own your data. At any time, you can:

  • Delete all local data by uninstalling the app or clearing app storage from your device settings.
  • Revoke a shared session at any time, which blocks the code and purges sensitive shared-session content from the relay.
  • Export your data as a JSON backup file at any time.
  • Request help with deletion through the support channel listed below.

To exercise any rights, contact us at the support URL above.

Security

  • Shared-session data is encrypted in transit using HTTPS.
  • The relay server accepts connections only over HTTPS.
  • Share codes are random, expire automatically, and can be revoked by the host.
  • Local data is stored in the browser's IndexedDB / Capacitor Preferences, which is sandboxed per-app on the device.
  • Olive does not retain voice recordings.

Health and safety

Olive is an informational timing and communication tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose labor or replace professional medical advice. Pattern notices, including the 5-1-1 pattern, are informational only. Follow the instructions of your healthcare professional, contact them before making medical decisions, and use local emergency services when urgent help is needed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated policy will be posted at the same location with a new effective date. Significant changes will be communicated within the app.

Contact

For questions, concerns, or data deletion requests:

  • Backend: relay.ashbi.ca
  • Developer: Available via the app's support channel
