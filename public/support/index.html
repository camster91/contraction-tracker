Olive support

Help with the Olive contraction timer, backups, privacy, or data deletion.

Contact

Email the independent developer with your device model, operating-system version, the Olive version shown in Settings, what you expected, and what happened. Do not include private pregnancy or medical information unless it is necessary for your request.

Email Olive support

This mailbox is the official Olive support and data-deletion channel. It is not an emergency or clinical service.

Urgent health concerns

Olive is a timing and communication tool, not an emergency or medical service. Follow your maternity care team's instructions. If you are worried or need urgent help, contact your care team or local emergency services rather than waiting for an app response.

Delete or export data

Open the backup tool to export a JSON backup. Uninstall Olive to remove its on-device data. Files and care summaries you previously exported remain wherever you saved or sent them; remove those copies separately.

Common troubleshooting

  • A timer was interrupted: reopen Olive and review the recovered-timer prompt. Resume or discard it explicitly.
  • Storage is full: export a backup if possible, free device storage, then retry the failed change.
  • Moving to a new device: export an Olive JSON backup from the old device and import it on the new one.
