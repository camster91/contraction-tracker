## Beta App Description

Olive is a private contraction timer and birth-journey organizer. Start and stop a timer, review history, add a missed contraction, and keep care details, questions and practical notes on your device. Export a backup or care summary when you choose. Core tools work offline and require no account.

Timing reminders are off by default. Enter instructions from your own care team before choosing to enable them. Olive reports observations and does not diagnose labor or replace professional care.

This beta is for evaluating the app with fabricated records. Native background behavior, file sharing and accessibility are under test. Do not rely on beta software as your only record. Keep an independent backup of anything important.

## Beta App Review Notes

No sign-in, reviewer account, purchase or subscription is required. Core timing and local records work offline. Support/privacy links open public websites, and exports go only to destinations selected by the user through the system share sheet.

For a basic run: launch Olive, tap Start, wait a few seconds, then Stop. Inspect the recorded entry. Use Add missed contraction to open the manual-entry form. Open Settings to inspect the disabled-by-default care-plan reminder; enabling it requires entering the user's own care-team instructions. Backup tools allow JSON export/import, and history provides a care summary.

The reminder reports recorded timing, not a diagnosis, risk assessment or direction to travel. The app does not connect to medical devices. Data is stored locally; there is no partner relay, account system, analytics, advertising, memory-book PDF or speech-recognition control in this release.
