# Olive product vision

Status: approved product direction. Last updated: 2026-08-08.

## North star

Olive is a calm, private coordination companion for the final weeks of
pregnancy, birth day, and the first 12 postpartum weeks. It helps the expecting
parent, partner, and care team stay aligned without attempting diagnosis or
replacing professional care.

The product should answer one question in a stressful moment:

> What does this family need to remember, record, share, or do next?

## Primary user

The expecting or recovering parent is the owner of the record and the primary
user. Olive should remain useful with one hand, at 3 a.m., without an account,
without connectivity, and without first configuring a family workspace.

Partners and invited support people are companion users. They can receive
specific responsibilities and see only the information the record owner
chooses to share. A care professional is a recipient of concise, factual
summaries—not an Olive account holder in the initial product.

## Product pillars

### Prepare

- Keep care-team contacts, hospital details, saved instructions, preferences,
  packing lists, questions, and support responsibilities together.
- Make the essential information available offline and easy to show or share.
- Let the parent decide what is complete, important, or private.

### Birth day

- Preserve Olive's one-tap contraction timer as the fastest primary action.
- Coordinate partner actions and lightweight status updates.
- Turn the family's own records into an objective care-team handoff.
- Keep all timing language observational and provider-configurable.

### Come home

- Provide a quiet timeline for appointments, recovery notes, questions, and
  practical support during the first 12 postpartum weeks.
- Help the parent remember what they want to discuss with a professional.
- Never score symptoms, recommend treatment, or present a medical all-clear.

### Remember

- Preserve a private birth timeline, notes, photos, milestones, and an
  exportable story.
- Keep keepsake features optional and visually secondary during labor.

## Product principles

1. **Calm beats comprehensive.** Show the next useful action, not a dashboard of
   everything the app knows.
2. **Local first.** Core preparation, timing, notes, and exports work without a
   network connection or account.
3. **Sharing is deliberate.** Nothing leaves the device until the owner creates
   a share, and every shared category is visible before sending.
4. **The parent remains in control.** Olive does not infer pregnancy status,
   diagnose labor, or silently change the care plan.
5. **Facts before interpretation.** Summaries report user-entered instructions,
   timestamps, durations, notes, and averages without clinical conclusions.
6. **Designed for reduced capacity.** Large targets, simple language, strong
   contrast, screen-reader support, and safe recovery from interruption are core
   requirements.
7. **Portable by default.** The complete record can be exported and restored in
   a documented format.

## Appropriate use of AI

AI may help organize the user's own information, draft a concise factual
summary, translate user-entered text, or improve voice and accessibility flows.
Every generated result must be reviewable before it is saved or shared.

AI must not:

- diagnose labor or interpret symptoms clinically;
- recommend medication, treatment, travel, or hospital admission;
- invent care instructions or fill missing health information;
- change pregnancy status, contact a person, or share data without confirmation;
- become necessary for timing, offline use, exports, or emergency access.

The initial v1.2 release does not require an AI model. Deterministic summaries
and excellent information architecture are the safer default.

## Explicit boundaries

Olive is not a fetal monitor, medical record, telehealth provider, symptom
checker, emergency dispatch service, baby-development authority, public social
network, advertising platform, or data marketplace.

## Success measures

Olive succeeds when:

- a new user records a contraction without instruction;
- a partner understands their next responsibility in under ten seconds;
- the parent can show or share essential care information while offline;
- a care-team summary contains objective, correctly scoped information;
- an interrupted action resumes without losing data;
- a user can understand exactly what will be shared and revoke it;
- postpartum use feels supportive without increasing cognitive load.
